<p>Dotboard puts a UK station departure board on your home screen, drawn dot by dot like an amber LED platform display.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Live departures for any UK station: time, destination, platform and expected time</li>
<li>"Exp 22:14", "Delayed" and "Cancelled", worded the way real boards show them</li>
<li>Scrolling calling points, alternating next departures and a ticking clock with seconds</li>
<li>Several boards on one home screen, each with its own station and an optional "calling at" filter</li>
<li>Resizable: bigger boards show more trains, and it adapts to both screens of a foldable</li>
<li>Amber, yellow-green or white LEDs, and a 12- or 24-hour clock</li>
<li>Battery-friendly: the animation and clock run in your launcher, so the app only wakes up to refresh departures</li>
<li>Works straight away: no sign-up or API key needed</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Live data</b></p>
<p>Live times come from the "Live Departure Board" feed on the Rail Data Marketplace (raildata.org.uk). By default they're fetched through Dotboard's small proxy, so you don't need an account or key. If you prefer, add your own free key in Settings and the app talks to the Rail Data Marketplace directly.</p>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no Google Play Services. To fetch departures Dotboard connects only to its proxy (which sees the station you ask for and your IP address, uses the IP briefly to prevent abuse, and keeps no logs) or, with your own key, directly to the Rail Data Marketplace.</p>
